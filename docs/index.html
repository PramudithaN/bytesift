Built For Everyone

Why you will love ByteSift.

No complicated settings, no steep learning curve. Just a clean tool that keeps your computer tidy.

🔍

Looks inside files, not just their names

Ever had a file called download_921.tmp or without any extension? ByteSift peeks at the real content inside so invoices, pictures, and notes always end up where they belong.

🗑️

Finds annoying duplicate downloads

Accidentally downloaded the same ticket or photo 4 times? ByteSift checks whether files are truly identical and groups copies into a separate Duplicates/ folder so you can free up space.

↩️

One-click Undo (Press Ctrl+Z)

Never worry about losing a file. ByteSift never deletes or overwrites anything. If you don't like how a folder was sorted, simply press Ctrl+Z (or ⌘Z on Mac) to put every file right back.

🔒

100% Private (No cloud, no accounts)

Your personal files stay strictly on your computer. ByteSift runs entirely in local memory without an internet connection, without third-party AI models, and with zero tracking.
